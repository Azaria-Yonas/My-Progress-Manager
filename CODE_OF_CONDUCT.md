# Code of Conduct

## My Goal
Since I uploaded this project for anyone to look at and use, I want to make sure any interactions here remain respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone—regardless of who you are, your background, or your coding experience level. 

If you are hanging out here, checking out the code, or opening an issue, just be decent to others.

## Rules of the Road

Here is what keeping things positive looks like:
* **Be kind:** Treat other people with respect and empathy.
* **Respect different viewpoints:** People have different experiences and ways of solving problems. Keep feedback constructive.
* **Own your mistakes:** If you pass along bad info or mess up, just apologize and learn from it.

What will not be tolerated:
* Trolling, insulting, or making personal attacks.
* Harassing anyone publicly or privately.
* Using sexualized language, imagery, or advances.
* Publishing anyone else's private information without permission.

## Enforcement (Just Me)
Since I run this project completely on my own, I am the one responsible for keeping this space healthy. 

If someone is being toxic, rude, or inappropriate, I will take action. This includes editing or deleting comments, closing issues, or blocking the user from the repository entirely. I don't have a corporate multi-step warning ladder—if someone is being genuinely harmful, I will just remove them.

## Reporting An Issue
If you see someone breaking these rules or making you feel unsafe in the discussions or issues here, please let me know privately by emailing me at **azariahyonas22@gmail.com**. I will look into it and handle it as soon as I can.
