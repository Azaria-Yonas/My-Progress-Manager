# Contributing Guidelines

Whats Up! How are yall doing? Thanks for checking out my project. 

Just to be completely transparent, this is a personal portfolio project of mine mostly just meant to showcase my own work, I’m generally not looking for outside contributions from the public, and I won't be actively managing a public queue of feature requests. 

However, here is how you can still interact with the project:

## Feel Free to Fork It!
You are 100% welcome to fork this repository. If you see something you want to tweak, improve, or use as a base for your own project, please feel free to fork it and build whatever you want on top of it!
